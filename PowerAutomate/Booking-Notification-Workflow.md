# Service Booking Notification Automation

## Proposed Workflow

1. Customer submits a booking request.
2. Power Automate detects the new request.
3. Booking details are retrieved.
4. An email notification is sent to the administrator.
5. The customer receives an acknowledgement.
6. The booking status is updated when reviewed.

## Suggested Connectors
- Microsoft Dataverse
- Office 365 Outlook
- Microsoft Teams

## Future Enhancements
- Automated booking reminders
- Cancellation notifications
- Service completion confirmation
- Customer feedback collection

## Implementation Status
Workflow design only.
No deployed or exported flow is included.
